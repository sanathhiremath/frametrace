The Pexels source page labels this video free to use. Pexels permits modified website/app use. This demonstration extracts sparse frames, resizes them to640×360, JPEG-encodes them, and adds analysis/annotation overlays in the viewer. It is not a stock-footage redistribution service, and no endorsement by depicted people, businesses, CityXcape, or Pexels is implied. The original video is downloaded locally and excluded from Git; the modified replay frames retain this attribution.

The independent source-review annotations are a small, single-annotator diagnostic set authored before running either detector. They are not an official Pexels benchmark or a multi-annotator ground truth dataset.

Source URLs and the exact local weight hashes are recorded for provenance. No model was trained or fine-tuned for this demonstration.
